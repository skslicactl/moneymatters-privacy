❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How do I add my investments?
Navigate to Advanced Settings → Holdings Management. You can add individual assets or use CSV import for bulk uploads.

Which markets are supported?
Indian Markets: NSE, BSE stocks and mutual funds
Global Crypto: Bitcoin, Ethereum, and major cryptocurrencies
International: Global stock markets
Is my financial data secure?
Yes! All data is stored locally on your device. We don't upload your portfolio information to any cloud services.

Can I import my existing portfolio?
Absolutely! Use the CSV import feature to bulk upload your existing investments from spreadsheets or other apps.

How accurate is the real-time data?
We use reliable market data sources for live price updates. Stock prices, mutual fund NAVs, and crypto prices are updated in real-time.

Can multiple family members use the app?
Yes, the app supports multiple user profiles with completely separate data for each family member.

What themes are available?
Choose from 7 professional themes: Midnight Blue, Forest Green, Royal Purple, Sunset Orange, Rose Gold, Ocean Teal, and Charcoal Gray - each with light and dark variants.

How do I calculate my financial health?
The app automatically calculates your financial health score based on emergency funds, debt ratio, savings rate, diversification, age-appropriate allocation, and liquidity.

🔧 Troubleshooting
Data Not Loading:
Check your internet connection for real-time updates
Refresh the app by pulling down on portfolio screens
Verify correct stock symbols and mutual fund codes
CSV Import Issues:
Ensure CSV format matches the template
Check for correct column headers
Verify asset symbols are valid (NSE/BSE for stocks, ISIN for mutual funds)
Performance Issues:
Close and restart the app
Ensure adequate storage space
Update to the latest version
Theme/Display Problems:
Try switching themes in Advanced Settings
Check device display settings
Restart the app after theme changes
